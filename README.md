<br clear="right" />

**Experience over ticket closure.** Delivering the story is the baseline, not the goal. I keep pushing on flows, edge cases, and form ergonomics until the feature is genuinely usable for the person on the other side of it.

**Deliberate, continuous research.** I make time to evaluate new frameworks, language features, and tooling — then bring back only what earns its place in production code.

**Performance treated as a feature.** Latency and responsiveness are requirements, not afterthoughts. I profile and measure before tuning, from query plans and virtual threads on the server to bundle size and change detection in the browser.

**Technical debt paid down on purpose.** I stay with what I ship: refactoring rough edges, tightening tests, and fixing the awkward parts left behind instead of routing around them.

**Spec, tests, and docs before the story.** I write the intended behaviour down first — a short spec, the tests that would prove it, and the notes a teammate would need — then implement. That order keeps scope honest and review straightforward.

**Best practices with a modern touch.** I follow current conventions rather than cargo-culting old ones: Conventional Commits for history that reads as a changelog, and the patterns that fit today's Spring and Angular rather than last year's defaults.
